🔍 Extracted Homework Problem

"Question 5: Solve the equation 3x + 9 = 24. Show your working steps."

Teacher Copilot Guidance

Let's break this down together! The goal is to isolate the variable x on one side of the equals sign.

  1. Step 1: Get rid of the constants. We have a +9 on the left. To balance, subtract 9 from both sides of the equation:
    3x + 9 - 9 = 24 - 9 => 3x = 15
  2. Step 2: Isolate the x. Since x is multiplied by 3, we divide both sides by 3 to find its value:
    3x / 3 = 15 / 3 => x = 5

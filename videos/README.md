# Video Lessons Directory

Place your curriculum video files here to make them permanently available in the **Teacher Login → Classroom Video Lessons** module.

## Directory Structure:
```text
videos/
├── class6/
│   ├── science/
│   ├── mathematics/
│   ├── social_science/
│   ├── english/
│   └── tamil/
├── class7/
│   ├── science/
│   ├── ...
├── class8/
│   ├── ...
├── class9/
│   ├── ...
└── class10/
    ├── science/
    │   └── nervous_system.mp4
    ├── mathematics/
    ├── social_science/
    ├── english/
    └── tamil/
```

Alternatively, teachers can click the **"Provide Video File"** or **"Select Video File from Computer"** button directly on the webpage to instantly play any video file (MP4, WebM, OGG, MKV) from their computer without copying it manually.
